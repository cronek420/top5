Universal Agentic Workforce

A reusable framework for businesses that need more than one automation. It organizes research, verification, routine actions, monitoring, reporting and human approval into one operating model.

Configure one for my businessBack to Universal Agents

What the workforce does

Find

Research customers, companies, opportunities, records, content, or other business inputs.

Verify

Check identity, data quality, fit, duplicates, confidence and business rules before taking action.

Act + escalate

Handle approved routine work automatically and send exceptions or consequential decisions to a person.
